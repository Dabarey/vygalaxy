Privacy Policy
Last updated: June 2026 · GDPR & UK GDPR compliant
Data controller: GALAXY Platform · vino@galaxyvy.com
1. What data we collect
  • Account data — name, email, password (hashed), profile photo, bio
  • Content — posts, messages, product listings you create
  • Payment data — transaction amounts and dates (card details processed by Stripe — we never see them)
  • Identity verification (KYC) — legal name, date of birth, country, ID document images (required for payouts)
  • Usage data — pages visited, features used (analytics cookies, only with consent)
2. Why we process it (legal basis)
  • Contract performance — providing your account, subscriptions, payouts
  • Legal obligation — KYC / AML regulations, financial record-keeping (7 years)
  • Legitimate interest — fraud prevention, platform security
  • Consent — analytics cookies (withdrawable any time)
3. Who we share data with
  • Stripe — payment processing (US, Privacy Shield / SCCs)
  • PayPal — payouts to creators
  • Cloudflare — infrastructure, CDN, Worker compute (EU & US)
  • Supabase — authentication and database (EU region)
  • We never sell your data. We never share it with advertisers.
4. Your rights (GDPR Art. 15–22)
  • Access — request a copy of all your data
  • Portability — download your data as JSON
  • Rectification — update incorrect data in your profile
  • Erasure — delete your account (financial records retained 7 years by law)
  • Restrict processing — contact us to pause processing
  • Object — opt out of analytics at any time
5. Data retention
Account data is kept while your account is active. On deletion, personal data is anonymised immediately. Financial records are retained for 7 years to comply with tax law. KYC documents are deleted within 30 days of account closure.
6. Cookies

Essential cookies — login session, CSRF protection. Cannot be disabled.

Analytics cookies — anonymous usage stats. Only set with your consent.

You can change your preference at any time using the buttons in section 4.

7. Contact & complaints
Email vino@galaxyvy.com for any data requests. We respond within 30 days. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your national supervisory authority (e.g. ICO in the UK, your national DPA in the EU).
